# Architecture

Market data is the single snapshot writer and control-plane owner. Each update identifies one changed underlying and carries the current eight-underlying spot vector. The pricer is the sole result producer, preallocates its SoA universe and output/gather buffers, computes only instruments belonging to the changed underlying, and publishes batches of up to 64 results. Fixed workers own stable index shards and are created once. Risk is the sole ring consumer and multiplies every Greek by position quantity before updating fixed per-underlying arrays and portfolio totals.

The result ABI also carries service PIDs, heartbeats, active kernel, worker and scheduling outcomes, batch/option counts, gaps, drops, depth, and high-water state. Market data reads this region without assuming ownership to build an aggregated status response. Startup is publisher, pricer, risk; shutdown is requested over the socket and propagated through shared flags. Region creators unlink during normal teardown, while attachers only unmap and close. `helix_demo` forks all three services with unique endpoints, asserts healthy live metrics, verifies safe reload and immutable-field rejection, shuts down, and checks every child exit code.

The build exposes separate static libraries for numerical/pricing functionality, worker concurrency, and Linux/POSIX orchestration. ABI v3 is published through `helix/posix/abi_v3.hpp` (with the v2 include retained as a source-compatibility shim) and has standard-layout, lock-free atomic, alignment, payload-size, and trivially-copyable assertions. The demo resolves binaries beside its own executable and uses `fork` plus `execv`; it captures and labels child output, enforces readiness deadlines, detects early exits, and coordinates teardown.
